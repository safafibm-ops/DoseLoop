DoseLoop: a passive H₂S dosimeter wristband read by a phone camera

One reusable wristband and one disposable colour-changing pod per worker. Scan the pod at the start and end of each shift and the app logs that worker's H₂S dose in ppm·hr.

2. Our solution

One product: reusable wristband plus a 30-day clip-in pod, scanned at every shift start and end.

Product hero image
(placeholder)

How a shift works

  1. Open the shutter and scan the pod at shift start.
  2. Work the shift; the ink darkens with H₂S dose.
  3. Scan again at shift end. Dose = end scan minus start scan.
  4. Close the shutter off-shift; any change is flagged.
